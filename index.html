About

Quality software shouldn't be a luxury. Robust Labs exists to democratize access to powerful, well-crafted applications — delivering them at near cost so that anyone, anywhere can benefit.

Robust Labs LLC was founded in 2026 by Roba Adnew in Brooklyn, New York, with a straightforward conviction: the best software tools are often locked behind price tags that exclude the people who need them most. Robust Labs set out to change that.

Robust Labs specializes in building AI-powered applications across productivity, education, and gaming — leveraging large language models and modern development practices to create software that is both intelligent and intuitive. The focus is on shipping real products that solve real problems, not chasing trends.

As a registered LLC in the state of New York, Robust Labs operates with the discipline and accountability of a professional software company while maintaining the speed and creativity of an independent studio.

Approach

AI-First Development

Large language models are integrated at the core of every product — not as a gimmick, but as fundamental infrastructure that makes each application smarter and more useful over time.

Ship, Measure, Iterate

Robust Labs builds in focused cycles, releasing early to real users and refining based on actual usage patterns. Every feature earns its place through demonstrated value.

Accessible by Default

Cost should never be a barrier to quality software. The stack and operations are engineered to keep costs low, passing savings directly to users.

Privacy-Conscious

Robust Labs collects only what's necessary, is transparent about data use, and gives users control over their information. Trust is foundational to everything built here.

Leadership

Roba Adnew

Founder & CEO

Roba brings over eight years of experience in product management, data science, and technology strategy. Before founding Robust Labs, he served as a Partner Product Manager at Meta, where he led commerce product strategy and grew a new native commerce platform from $0 to $600M in revenue within eight months. Prior to that, he spent four years as a Data Science Partner at Meta, leading measurement and analytics programs and collaborating with cross-functional teams on advertising and product development.

Roba holds a B.S. in Mathematics and a B.A. in Economics from the University of Maryland, College Park. His background in quantitative analysis, product strategy, and go-to-market execution informs how Robust Labs approaches building software — data-driven, user-focused, and designed for real impact.

He is also the cofounder of Agency Dante, a consultancy advising diverse- and women-led startups on brand and launch strategy, and a founding member of Elevate by Facebook, an employee-led program he helped grow from grassroots initiative to executive-sponsored program reaching millions of small business owners.
